Quick start
===========

1. Open one or more files with **File → Open…** or drag-and-drop them
   directly onto the window (CSV, TXT, NPY, and NPZ are supported).

2. In the **Data & Series** tab each row represents one independent series.
   Use the **X** and **Y** drop-downs to pick columns — they can come from
   different files as long as the row counts match.
   Choose **Line**, **Scatter**, or **Histogram** from the type combo.

3. Hit **Plot** (or leave *Auto* checked to re-plot on every change).

4. Switch to the **Plot Style** or **Axes & Labels** tabs to adjust grid,
   log scale, limits, fonts, and annotations.

5. Go to the **Export** tab to save the figure as PNG, PDF, or SVG at any DPI.

The tabs are icon-only to reduce clutter — hover any tab to see its name.

That's it.  Everything else — error bars, curve fitting, computed columns,
templates — is optional and available whenever you need it.
